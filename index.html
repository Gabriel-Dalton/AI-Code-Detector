    Heuristics & weights

    Each signal contributes its weight when matched. The score is the sum of matched weights over the sum of absolute weights, clamped to 0–100. Positive weights are AI tells; the single negative weight — Human-style complexity — is a counterweight that pulls the score down when a snippet shows genuine human variety.
