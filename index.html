Most samples are curated — distilled from common LLM idioms so each one cleanly demonstrates one or two signals. A few are verbatim transcripts captured from popular LLMs to keep a real-world feel. Each badge tells you which. Click Load into analyzer on any card to see how the detector scores it.

Heuristics & weights

Each signal contributes its weight when matched. The verdict score is normalized over the sum of absolute weights and clamped to 0–100. Positive weights are AI tells; the negative-weighted Human-style complexity heuristic acts as a counterweight when a snippet shows real human variety.
